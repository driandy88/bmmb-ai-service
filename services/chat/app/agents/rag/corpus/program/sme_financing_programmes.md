This document sets out the SME financing programmes and their general terms.

## 1. Introduction / Pengenalan

Bank Muamalat Malaysia Berhad (BMMB) offers Shariah-compliant financing
facilities for small and medium enterprises incorporated in Malaysia. All
facilities described here are structured under Islamic contracts approved by the
Bank's Shariah Committee. Semua kemudahan adalah patuh Syariah.

These terms apply to applications submitted through any BMMB channel, including
the SME financing portal, branch counters, and the digital assistant.

## 2. Programmes / Program Pembiayaan

### 2.1 SME Term Financing-i

A term facility for business expansion, asset acquisition, and renovation of
business premises. Structured under Tawarruq (Commodity Murabahah).

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Financing amount | RM50,000 – RM5,000,000 |
| Tenure | 1 – 10 years |
| Profit rate | From 5.75% p.a. (floating, pegged to BMMB Base Rate) |
| Security | Property, machinery, or CGC guarantee |
| Early settlement | Permitted; no compensation charge on the unearned profit |

Suitable for businesses with at least two years of operating history and audited
or management accounts for the most recent financial year.

### 2.2 SME Working Capital Financing-i

A revolving facility covering day-to-day operational needs — inventory purchase,
payroll, and supplier payments. Structured under Tawarruq with a 12-month
revolving line reviewed annually.

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Financing amount | RM30,000 – RM2,000,000 |
| Tenure | 12 months, renewable |
| Profit rate | From 6.25% p.a. |
| Security | Unsecured up to RM300,000; above that, collateral or CGC cover |
| Drawdown | Multiple drawdowns permitted within the approved limit |

Kemudahan ini sesuai untuk perniagaan yang memerlukan modal kerja bermusim.

### 2.3 SME Equipment & Machinery Financing-i

Asset-backed financing for the purchase of new or reconditioned machinery,
commercial vehicles, and production equipment. Structured under Ijarah Muntahia
Bittamleek (lease ending in ownership) or Murabahah, depending on the asset.

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Financing amount | Up to 90% of the asset invoice value, max RM3,000,000 |
| Tenure | 1 – 7 years, not exceeding the asset's useful life |
| Profit rate | From 5.95% p.a. |
| Security | The financed asset, registered in the Bank's name until settlement |

Reconditioned assets must not exceed five years of age at the point of
application.

## 3. Eligibility / Kelayakan

### 3.1 Business criteria

The applicant must be a business registered in Malaysia under the Companies Act
2016 or the Registration of Businesses Act 1956, with at least 51% Malaysian
ownership. Perniagaan mestilah berdaftar di Malaysia.

Minimum operating history is 24 months for term and working capital facilities,
and 12 months for equipment financing where the asset itself serves as security.

### 3.2 Financial criteria

The business must show a minimum annual sales turnover of RM100,000 and a
positive net operating profit in the most recent financial year. The debt
service coverage ratio, computed as net operating income divided by total
financing obligations, must be at least 1.25 times after including the facility
being applied for.

Directors and guarantors must have no active bankruptcy proceedings and no
adverse CCRIS record showing more than two months in arrears within the
preceding twelve months.

### 3.3 Exclusions

Applications are declined where the business derives income from activities that
are not Shariah-compliant, where the business is under receivership or winding-up
proceedings, or where a previous BMMB facility was written off and remains
unsettled.

## 4. Required documents / Dokumen diperlukan

All applicants must submit:

- SSM registration documents — Form 9, Form 24, Form 49, or the Superform for
  entities registered after 2016
- Company Constitution or Memorandum & Articles of Association, where applicable
- Audited financial statements for the two most recent financial years
- Bank statements for the six most recent months, all operating accounts
- Directors' and guarantors' MyKad, front and back
- Board resolution authorising the financing application
- Latest tax return (Form C or Form B) and tax computation

Equipment financing applications additionally require the supplier quotation or
proforma invoice, and the asset specification sheet.

Dokumen yang tidak lengkap akan melambatkan proses permohonan.

## 5. Fees and charges / Yuran dan caj

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Processing fee | 0.5% of the approved amount, minimum RM500 |
| Stamp duty | 0.5% of the financing amount, per the Stamp Act 1949 |
| Legal and valuation fees | At cost, borne by the customer |
| Late payment charge | 1% per annum on the overdue amount (ta'widh) |
| Facility review fee | RM300 per annual review, revolving facilities only |

Ta'widh is compensation for actual loss and is not compounded. It is not treated
as income to the Bank beyond the Bank's actual cost, in line with the Shariah
Committee's ruling on late payment.

## 6. Application process / Proses permohonan

An application moves through five stages: submission, document verification,
credit assessment, approval, and disbursement. Indicative turnaround from
complete submission to decision is ten working days for facilities up to
RM500,000 and fifteen working days above that.

The Bank may request additional information at any stage. An indicative
eligibility result produced by a digital channel is not an approval — final
approval rests with the Bank's credit committee.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Can I apply if my business is less than two years old?**
Term and working capital facilities require 24 months of operating history.
Equipment financing may be considered from 12 months where the asset provides
security. Businesses below these thresholds are referred to the SME financing
team, who can advise on alternative schemes.

**Is collateral always required?**
No. Working capital facilities up to RM300,000 may be approved unsecured,
subject to credit assessment. Above that threshold, collateral or Credit
Guarantee Corporation cover is required.

**What happens if I settle early?**
Early settlement is permitted on all facilities. Unearned profit is rebated
(ibra') and no compensation charge applies.

**Bolehkah saya memohon lebih daripada satu kemudahan?**
Ya. Multiple facilities may be held concurrently, provided the combined debt
service coverage ratio remains at or above 1.25 times.
